> D means domain, R means range. 

Let f be a 1 to 1 function with D = A, R = B, 
then $D_{f^1} = R_f$, $R_{f^1} = D_f$

$f^{-1}(y) = x \iff f(x) = y$ for any y in B
## Cancelation formulas 
$f^{-1}f(x) = x, \forall x \in A$
$f^{-1}f(x) = x, \forall x \in B$

What are the cancelation formulas for inverse functions
?
$f^{-1}f(x) = x, \forall x \in D$
$f^{-1}f(x) = x, \forall x \in R$


How could you use the cancelation formulas to show g(x) is the inverse of f(x)?
? 
Since $f^-1(f(x)) =  x$ by cancelation formula, we can show that $g(f(x))$ is x.



Why is the solution to $\sin^{-1}(\sin(9\pi/4)) \neq 9\pi/4$ ?
?
The domain of $\arcsin$ is $[-1, 1]$, and 9pi/4 is NOT in the domain, so we have to do some funny business.
since sin(9pi/4) = sin(pi/4) by periodicity (9pi/4 = 2pi  + 1pi/4) we have $\sin^{-1}(\sin(1\pi/4))$. By the cancelation formula, we get the answer of pi/4!
