## How can $|3a-6|$ be we written as a piecewise function?
>[!Answer]-
>$|3a-6|= \begin{cases} 3a - 6 & 3a - 6 \geq 0 \\ -(3a - 6) & 3a - 6 < 0 \end{cases}$
>
>3a - 6 >= 0 when a = 2
>3a - 6 < 0 when a < 2
>So,
>$|3a-6|= \begin{cases} 3a - 6 & a \geq 2 \\ 6 - 3a & a < 2 \end{cases}$

## How to expand $|x| \geq a$ and $|x| \leq a$
>[!Answer]-
>$|x| \geq a \Longrightarrow x \geq a\ or\ x\leq -a$
>$|x| \leq a \Longrightarrow -a \leq x \leq a$
