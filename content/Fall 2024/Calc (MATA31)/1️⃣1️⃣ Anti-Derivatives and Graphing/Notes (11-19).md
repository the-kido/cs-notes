## Antiderivatives

Let's say the anti-derivative of $f(x)$ is $F(x)$. By finding 1 antiderivative of f(x), say g(x), we can apply the above Corollary to find that F(x) = g(x) + C describes *all* anti-derivatives! 

E.g. Find all antiderivatives of f(x) = 2x.
First, we know that one of them is F(x) = x^2 since F'(x) = f(x). By applying the corollary, we get that all antiderivatives can be described by F(x) = 2x + c for some c in R
