<span class="definition">Least upper bound property</span>
- Every non-empty set of real numbers that is bounded above has a least upper bound 
- Also called "The Completeness Axiom"
	- basically saying the set of R is "complete" in a sense; not sure what that means
- This is NOT true for $\mathbb{Q}$
	- "Every non-empty subset of $\mathbb{Q}$ that is bounded above has a least upper bound in $\mathbb{Q}$" is a false statement.
- $S = \{x \in \mathbb{Q} | x^2 \leq 2 \}$
	- Saying $sup(\sqrt{ 2 })$ would not be right since it is irrational!
