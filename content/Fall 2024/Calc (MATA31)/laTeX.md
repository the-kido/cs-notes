It's very interesting, and this will go over some of the important symbols and whatnots

To start, you use \$$ !
and end with
\$$

\\frac allows you to make a fraction given two parameters with curly brackets --> {}

>[!example]
==\$$ \frac{1}{2}== $$ \frac{1}{2} $$

For exponents, you use ^ and for subscripts you use \_.  If you need to add several characters after one of those, just surround everything after the ^ or \_ with {}

>[!example] 
$$ value_{something \space down \space there}^{something \space up \space here} $$


As an exercise, let us use the formula for finding the Molar Enthalpy of a reaction given all the Formation Enthalpy's 

$$ 
\Delta H_r=\sum n (\Delta H_f^\circ \space products) -\sum n (\Delta H_f^\circ \space reactants)
$$
