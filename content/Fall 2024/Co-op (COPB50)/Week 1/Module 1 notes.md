### Labour Market Information (LMI) 
>[!question] What is LMI?
Trends and stats that show the outlook of, trends in, and influences of a job. 

This information includes:
 - Job outlooks and projected number of job openings
 - Annual number of job postings 
 - Job growth 
 - Pay 
 - Education and training pathways
 - In-demand skills and knowledge 
 - Industries within which jobs are found 
 - Employment characteristics 
 - Occupations held by graduates from a field of study

>[!question] What are LMI's good for?
>Use LMI's to ==figure out what skills and experience you need==, trends that might impact employment, etc. 
>
>For example, using LMI's I can figure out the soft skills most valued in CS (like problem-solving skills) or when the market is down

## Job Posting Analysis / Evaluation
~*The art of dissecting job job postings*~

>[!danger] MAKE SURE TO READ YOUR JOB POSTINGS CAREFULLY!!!
>- It's important you know crucial details about the role and its requirements + if the job suits you or not

## Parts of a Job Posting
The sections to focus on are the
- Role description
- Qualifications section
- Company description
- Responsibilities section 

- It's important to understand the company’s values
	- If they explicitly mention it, pay special attention.
- Tailor cover letters and resumes to align with the job requirement / role description
	-  Use keywords in your resume (especially to get past automated systems)
	- Reflect the language you use to align with employer's needs. 
		- If the job posting has themes of excitement, then be excited in your cover letter. If there are themes of diversity, then create a tone which doesn't sound *mechanical* in your resume to show you care. 
- Know what you're getting into (your job duties)

### Aligning experience to job requirements
T-analysis is the best way to go here. Analyze keywords/phrases/language from the role description and see how your experience matches those. 

>[!example] Here is an example

| Keywords/ Phrases/ Language                   | Matches                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Obtaining informed consent from parents       | I have actually done this while working at a camp counsellor when obtaining trip permission forms.                         |
| Explaining Questionnaire packages to students | I have done this at Student Council as well as at camp.                                                                    |
| Quality Assurance                             | There were elements of this when mixing drinks at Starbucks.                                                               |
| Contributing to a Clinic Environment          | I don’t have this yet. This is why I want the job, to get this. I will have some by next year when I take the lab courses. |
