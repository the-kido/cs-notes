Product rule:
- Multiply by # of choices to get total choices
- doesn't apply if previous choices effect future choices 
- 'and' = '$\times$'

for a series of things that could be included/excluded (i.e. subsets) you can mulitply the # of things with the # of choices. So a set of sine 4 will have $2^4$ subsets.

Inclusion/exclusion rule (subtraction rule specifically)
- if a task can be done n_1 ways or in n_2 ways but have n ways in common, then there are n_1 + n_2 - n ways to do the task.
