R-permutations: $\frac{n!}{(n-r)!}$
R-combinations: $\frac{n!}{r! * (n-r)!}$
- e.g. # of size-3 subsets for A,B,C,D, & E. Order doesn't matter.
	- C(5,3) = 6
	- Each permutation of ABC will be counted 6 more times than it should be since order doesn't matter, so we divide by 6. Since any permutation will have that same thing happen, we apply division rule.
