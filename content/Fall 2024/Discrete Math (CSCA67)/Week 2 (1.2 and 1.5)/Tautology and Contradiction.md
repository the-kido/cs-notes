<span class="definition">Tautology</span> A formula that's always true
- We aim for these in proofs!

<span class="definition">Contradiction</span> A formula that's always false

>[!example] Here are examples of tautologies, contradictions, or neither
>P ∨ (Q ∨ ¬P) <-- Always true
>P ∧ ¬(Q ∨ ¬Q) <-- Always false
>P ∨ ¬(Q ∨ ¬Q) <-- True and false
### Tautology Laws
1. $P \vee (tautology)$ eqv P
2. $P \wedge (tautology)$ is a tautology
3. $\neg (tautology)$ is a contradiction
### Contradiction Laws
1. $P \vee (contradiction)$ is a contradiction
2. $P \wedge (contradiction)$ eqv P
3. $\neg (contradiction)$ is a tautology
