$A \subseteq B$ $\equiv \forall x, x \in A \to x \in B$
>The negation of the above would be: 
$\neg \forall x, x \in A \to x \in B$
$\equiv \exists x, x \in B \wedge x \not \in B$
$\equiv A \not \subseteq B$
