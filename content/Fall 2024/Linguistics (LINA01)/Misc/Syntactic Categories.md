> Also called lexical categories/ parts of speech

- We have nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, determiners, degree words, auxiliary verbs, and negation\
- There are two sub-categories that separate them:
	- Lexical category (Open Class)
		- Nouns (N)
		- Verbs (V)
		- Adjectives (A/Adj)
		- Adverbs (Adv)
		- Prepositions (Prep)
	- Functional Category (Closed Class)
		- Determiners (Det)
		- Negation (Neg)
		- Degree Words (Deg)
		- Auxiliary verbs (Aux)
