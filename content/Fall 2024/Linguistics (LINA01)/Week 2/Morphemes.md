<span class="definition">Morphemes</span> The smallest unit of meaning in a word carrying some information about either function or the meaning.
- Some examples are `box`, `-ish`, and `fancy`, and `un-`
- We have an intuition over what morphemes can connect to what
	- combining `dis-` and `computer` and `-ed` gets us `discomputered` o_o

Morphemes can be "words" (<span class="emphasis">free-standing</span> morphemes) or a part of a word (`-s`, `-ly`, `-ish`, etc.)


### Free and Bound Morphemes
- Morphemes can either be free or bound 

<span class="definition">Free(-standing) Morphemes</span>
- A  morpheme that can stand on its own. 
- sit, charge, note, glass, etc.

<span class="definition">Bound Morphemes</span>
- Must be attached to another morpheme or word.
- `-ing`, `de-`, `-ism`, etc.
- They are usually affixes
