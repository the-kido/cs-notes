<span class="definition">Syntactic Categories</span>(or <span class="emphasis">parts of speech</span>) are words that share significant grammatical characteristics. Nouns, adjectives, and pronouns are syntactic categories.
- `The romly poys whemmed two noolish fronks.` can still be somewhat deciphered because we can instinctively tell what words fall under what syntactic category (i.e. "`romly`" is an adjective; "`poys`" is a noun, and "`whemmed`" is a verb)
