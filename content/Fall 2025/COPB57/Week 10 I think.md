Option 2: Transitioning into the Workplace 

I managed to get hired! However I'm not working at a private company, but instead the Ontario government. I don't expect the culture to be that of a small, close-knit team, but instead something a lot more formal, pragmatic, and utilitarian, which admittedly I am not excited for, but I am keeping my mind open. (It could be totally different from what I imagine for all I know.)

I fundamentally hope that I can work with people well and learn new skills while in a professional environment, and so the pragmatic culture lends itself very well for those goals, which is a big upside. Alternatively, working in a more laid back, less bureaucratic culture is my **end** goal throughout my career, simply because I think it would be the most fun and it's where I feel I would thrive. However it's good to compare and contrast with these two polar opposites with hands-on experience, starting with working under OPS.

I plan to adapt from my student to professional life by asking a lot of questions (I will be speaking to AA&CC about just this topic soon), trying to feel confident with myself, and faking a more "professional" demeanor until I can actually make it. I am already adjusting my sleep to make sure I can accommodate communicating every day in the morning. 

Thinking of challenges is easy when you're stressing and paranoid about this topic ever since your acceptance letter! For instance, will I be able to fit into the team's culture? How closely will I be looked over? What if I'm not experienced enough with what we're using to actually be of help? Of course, I got accepted for a reason, but these are my doubts. I am confident in my ability to control my work-life balance. I do well with school as is. Work is just school but more commuting, that of which I enjoy.

2. Work towards developing more realistic and consistent sleeping habits (helps with life in general, but is especially important while working!)
3. Be confident that I can do well even if I am not too sure what I have to do now until Jan 5
