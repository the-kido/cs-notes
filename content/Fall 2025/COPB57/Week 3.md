2.
One of the choices I made were to actively choose longer work terms instead of the 4, 8 month terms most people prefer. Objectively speaking it has its pros, like being able to gain more experience and better integrate into the workplace, but subjectively I just like the idea of not going to school for more than 8 months 😅. When considering which option to pick, I consulted with lots of upper years I knew, as well as people in the same year as me who have done the same research. I concluded that 4 to 8 months is ideal, but a 12 or 16 month position still has its benefits and I was more emotionally inclined to work longer. Also, longer positions are applied to less, so I would give myself an advantage there. Looking back, although I haven't yet experienced the fruits of my decision yet, I still stick by it even if I am in the minority amongst my peers
