Define $K$ as a (set of attr's) for a relation $R$. Then $K$ is a key if:
1. K --> All attributes of R
	1. Keys define *uniqueness* of a relation
2. No subset of $K$ lets the above be true (in that $K$ is **minimal**)
	- For instance, if $AG \to BCDE$, but $A \to BCDE$, then $AG$ is a *super key* and $A$ is the actual key
