4NF stuffs

Relations are "sets"
- Remember sets don't have repeating elements

In order to mutate these relations, we can use operators:
- Union/intersection/difference
- Selection
- "Projection" (which picks certain columns)
- Products and joins (Composing relations in useful ways)
	- Like the opposite of normalising. Normalising splits big relations into smaller ones. 

There are a bunch of symbols to represent different operators
- These are defined for our "relational algebra"

Most operators require both inputs (tables/relations) have the *same schema*
- Like for union/intersection/difference


Projection takes an L list of attributes (column names( and gives only those columns
$\pi_\text{film-type}(\text{Movies})$ 
- Where Movies is a table whose tuples have a title, year, length, film-type, or studio-name, the above table will only be the column with the film name.

Selection takes a condition (that involves the attributes) and returns the tuple(s?) that satisfy it.

Cartesian Product pairs all tuples of the first table with all tuples of the seconds
- Means the output will be $m \times n$ in size!
- The tuples don't need to be the same schema
