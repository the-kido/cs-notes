## Continuous


| Distribution                             | Use Case                                                                                                                                                                                       | Extra Notes                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Uniform $X \sim \text{Uniform}(l, u)$    | Uses a bounded interval of the real line. Is uniform because only the "length" of the interval matters (you can shift the interval from $[2, 5]$ to $[5, 8]$ and the chance is still the same) | The PMF is constant and the CDF is really simple.                                                                                          |
| Exponential $X \sim \text{Exp}(\lambda)$ | TIME! For the "time" until something happens.                                                                                                                                                  | $\lambda$ is the # of times a thing happens over a unit of time (say, 1 second). $1/\lambda$ is the average time the event takes to happen |
| Gamma $X \sim \text{Gamma}(a,\lambda)$   | Its memoryless! Time for $a$ occurrences to happen over a period of time.                                                                                                                      | Sum of exponential. Therefore sum of time for one occurrence.                                                                              |
| Normal $X \sim$ idk                      | No CDF or PMF.
