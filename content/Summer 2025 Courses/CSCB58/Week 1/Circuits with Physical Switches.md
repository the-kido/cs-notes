Before jumping into [[Transistors (pMOS and nMOS)]], we use physical switches as a stepping stone. Refer to [[Physical Representation of Boolean Values]] for how we know the output is Logic-0 or Logic-1
![[Physical Switch Circuit.png|300]]
- The physical switch, when opened, means the result has no voltage, yet it's connected to ground = 0V = Logic-0.
- When the physical switch is closed, then the current can flow; means 5Vs = Logic-0.
