
>[!note] The little bubble (called the ***inversion bubble***) at the end or start of a gate (update: transistors also use these; they are not gates #todo) signifies a "not". Think of whatever variable that goes into the bubble as "inverted". 
>
>This little quirk applies to the [[Logic Gates#NAND gates|"not" gates]], [[Logic Gates#Inverter|inverters]], [[Transistors (pMOS and nMOS)|transistors]], and more!
