Obvious axioms
- $0 \cdot 0 = 0$
- $1 \cdot 1 = 1$
- $0 \cdot 1 = 1 \cdot 0 = 0$
- If $x = 1$, then $\bar x = 0$
Extrapolated axioms:
- $x \cdot 0 = 0$
- $x \cdot 1 = x$
- $x\cdot x = x$
- $x \cdot \bar x = 0$
- $\bar{ \bar x} = x$ 
- $x + 1 = 1$
- $x + 0 = x$
- $x + x = x$
- $x + \bar x = 1$
Laws
- Commutative Law
	- $x \cdot y = y \cdot x$
	- $x + y = y + x$
- Associative
	- $x \cdot (y \cdot z) = (x \cdot y) \cdot z$
	- $x + (y + z) = (x+y) + z$
- Distributive
	- $x \cdot (y + z) = x \cdot y + x \cdot z$
	- $x + (y \cdot z) = (x + y) \cdot (x+ z)$
		- In "conventional algebra" (like literally $+$ and $\times$ from elementary) this law would make no sense. 
- ==Simplification Law==
	- $x + (\bar x \cdot y) = x + y$
	- $x \cdot (\bar x + y) = x\cdot y$
