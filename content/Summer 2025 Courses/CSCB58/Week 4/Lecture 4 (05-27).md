
#### T-flip flops (modified D-flip-flop)
- We just have an extra xor gate and pass the outputted $Q$ alongside $T$ 
- T = Toggle
- It toggles Q. But when T is low it preserves Q

JK Flip-Flops are like SR latches except they dont have the instability by defining what `11` does
