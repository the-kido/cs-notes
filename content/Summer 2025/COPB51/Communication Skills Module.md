# 1.2 Persuasive Communication
- Different people = different approaches to decision-making
- Different situations = different approaches as well
- Arguments should both be rational and emotional
	- Humor works well with emotional arguments
- Remember the audience
	- diverse audience = use objective *and* subjective means
- Really, remember the audience 

# 1.3 Presenting
- Presentations have beginnings, middles, and ends
	- Start: Hook and core message
		- Core message
		- Present Problem; Give Solution
	- Middle: Solution + proof
		- Or consequences to *not* do the thing
	- End: summary and Call to Action
- Visuals > Text
- Before: Practice and Time
- During: Engage the Audience, use non-verbal communication, speak good and at a good tone, and speak confidently

# 1.5 Non-verbal communication
- 99% of communication is unconscious (intuitive communication / honest signals).
- Types:
	- Paralinguistics (pitch, volume, speed, the way you regulate your voice as you speak)
	- Body language
	- Appearance (good grooming = seems more respectful)
	- Artifacts (physical things that indicate something about you)
		- I.e. figurines, the type of tech you use, etc.
- Emotional Intelligence:
	- Social awareness ("what do we know? What can we infer?")
