Summary

Even in the introduction of the module, emphasis is put on tailored resumes and understanding industry trends.


- effectively showcasing your qualifications = interviews
- more applications = more opportunity
- common screen-out reasons
	- Irrelevant skills
	- Lack of attention to detail
	- Not understanding the job posting / qualifications
	- Not using "specific" examples that lack technical language
- Conversion = probability of getting an interview based on your applications
	- "converting an application into an interview"
- 5 such factors that affect conversion include:
	- UofT exclusivity (applying to jobs exclusive to UofT limits your competition with other universities)
	- Connections
	- Applying to less applied positions / positions outside your industry
	- Applying outside of just the GTA
	- Applying to longer roles (since people don't like applying to them)
- All positions can be split into 4 "quadrants" on the `Competition to Conversion Matrix`.
	- High Conversion, Low Competition
		- Positions where you already have a connection or require extra steps (i.e. screening or code challenges) since those deter others from applying.
	- Low Conversion, Low Competition
		- Positions outside the GTA OR positions for a 12-16 month duration
		- Ig it's low conversion since you don't have any "advantage" over other applicants; you just have less competition to deal with.
	- High Conversion, High Competition
		- UofT exclusive positions
		- You have the advantage of competing with far less people, and the orgs WANT UofT students!
	- Low Conversion, High Competition
		- Big name companies
		- Short terms by high-profile orgs
- When applying, it's important to plan and set application targets. The goal is to improve your applications and ensure quality systematically.
- For external applications, we just fill out a form to share search activity.
What hiring managers look for:
1. Relevance
	1. Show how your skills relate to the job position directly by including keywords / phrases reflective of the job.
2. Proof of ability
	1. Demonstrating your ability to perform job functions
	2. Tip: avoid minimum qualifications and emphasize the key responsibilities
3. Consistency and Alignment
	1. Your cover letter, resume, etc. should present a consistent message. 
4. Verification of Qualifications
	1. Confirm you have the minimum qualifications 
5. Format / Clarity
	1. Use a simple layout
	2. Include concise accomplishment statements 
	3. Optimize for ATS

- Ensure your resume doesn't get overlooked by ATS!!1
