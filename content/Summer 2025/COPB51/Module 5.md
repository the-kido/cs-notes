ethics = basis for productivity and positivity!

Ethics is important to uphold in the workspace as it's the basis for productivity and positivity. The module describes 5 "core principles". Integrity is demonstrated though honesty (truthfulness), transparency (giving clarity about your situation through communication), and accountability (ensuring you stick to what you commit to). Fair Treatment promotes a positive work environment by being respectful of other's ideas, inclusive in teamwork (collaborating instead of competing), and respectfully giving / receiving constructive feedback. Confidentiality demonstrates responsibility and can be ensured by handling sensitive data securely, asking for permission (e.g. asking someone if you want to share someone else's work), and by protecting your digital information too. Lastly, respect, which ensures a workplace is healthy & inclusive, can be demonstrated by understanding many perspectives, not acting negatively, and by respecting other's boundaries as well.

As for inclusion in the workplace, it is the idea of creating a positive environment for everyone. This includes recognising diversity between all individuals no matter their differences. Beyond that, invoking a sense of belonging by making employees feel value, heard, and involved can result in more engagement and productivity too. This can be done through representation, inclusive policies, and more.
