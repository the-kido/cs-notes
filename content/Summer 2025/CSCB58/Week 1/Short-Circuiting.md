Happens when Resistance $R = 0$ because the impedance given by $I = \frac{V}{R}$ would be $I = \frac {V} 0 = \infty$ theoretically, which is bad news and will definitely burn your little circuit.

To prevent this, simply do not let $R$ become 0 at *any point*; if the path from $V_{cc}$ to ground is straight with no resistance, you *will* get a short-circuit.
