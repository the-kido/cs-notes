Let X and Y be numbers
$X + Y$ is addition... right yes
$X - Y$ is *subtraction*
$X + (-Y)$ is also subtraction!

Now the question is, how can we represent a positive Y as a negative number, assuming we have some input... eh lets call it $C$, that determines if Y is negative or not? 
The answer: [[Signed Bin Numbers (2's complement + sign-and-magnitude)|2's complement]]

So get the 2's complement of Y and add it. That is all!
