You should know how to do it geometrically or analytically (with math)

The things we'd need to figure out are:
- The a and b values (the interval of interest)
- The integrand (f)
- the $\Delta x$
- The sample point (right/left sum)

In order, you should figure out:
1. The $\Delta x$ 
2. What a and b could be. Make a guess based on observation
3. The sample point (it is most often "right")
4. The integrand 

 Tips:
 - There can be many answers. 
 - You have to make sure you haven't left any terms. No leftover terms or factors. 
 - You can try substituting everything back to get the original Riemann sum again 
 - Include "through observation" when reverse engineering whenever making some justifiable guesses.
