We did not go over this in class.

But notice that instantaneous velocity is the slope of the "distance" function.
I.e. if your distance looks like the graph $t^2$, your velocity is $2t$.

So if you want to get velocity, you would differentiate the displacement function.
To get the displacement from velocity, you would have to use integration!

i.e. $$\int_a^b v(t) = d(b) - d(a)$$
where a to b is the time that something moved.
