Calculate
$\int_0^1 (x^2\sqrt x + e^x + \frac{1}{x^2 + 1})$
We will use the FTOC.
First, we gotta ensure the hypotheses are satisfied
1. f(x) is continuous on [0, 1] since the first term is cont on R^+ and the other terms are cont on R. So it's defo cont on [0,1]
2. Now we need F(x)....
	1. $F(x) = \frac{1}{3.5}x^3.5 + e^x + tan^{-1}x$
So then the answer is $\left[ \frac{1}{3.5}x^{3.5} + e^x + tan^{-1}(x) \right]^1_0 \ =  -\frac{5}{7} + e + \frac \pi 4$
