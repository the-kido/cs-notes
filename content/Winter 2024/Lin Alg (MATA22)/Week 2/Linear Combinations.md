$$\text{a-sum-of-vectors-that-are-scaled} + \dots + \text{a-sum-of-vectors-that-are-scaled} = \text{a linear combination}$$

> [!defn]
> For a subset $S \subseteq V$, we define a linear combination of vectors from S to be:
> $$a_1v_1 + a_2v_2 + \dots a_kv_k = \sum_{i=1}^k a_iv_i$$
> where $v_i \in S$ and $a_i \in F$. We say $\sum_{i=1}^k a_iv_i$ is a linear combination of ${v_1, v_2, \dots, v_k}$.
> - $k$ is the number of vectors in $S$.
