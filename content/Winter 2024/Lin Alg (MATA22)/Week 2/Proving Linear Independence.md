>[!example] Check that $\set{sin(x) , cos(x) }$ is linearly independent.
>Suppose $a\sin(x) + b\cos(x) = 0$ for all $x \in \mathbb R$ and $a,b \in \mathbb R$.
>Consider when $x = \pi/2$
>> Then $a\cdot 1 + b\cdot 0 = 0$
>> $a = 0$
>
>Consider when $x = 0$
>>Then $a\cdot 0 + b\cdot 1 = 0$
>>$b = 0$.
>
>So a = b = 0, meaning the set is definitely independent
