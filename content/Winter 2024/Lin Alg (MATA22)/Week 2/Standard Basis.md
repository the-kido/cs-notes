>[!defn]  Standard Basis of $\mathbb R^n$
>The n vectors below are the standard basis of $\mathbb R^n$ (C work too tho)
>$$\set{ (1,0,\dots,0), (0,1,\dots,0), (0,0,\dots,1)   } $$
>
>>[!check]- Proof
>>(for the sake of example, let n = 3)
>>So the basis vectors are (0,0,1), (0,1,0), and (1,0,0)
>>Suppose we had linear dependence.
>>x(1,0,0)+ y(0,1,0) + z(0,0,1) = (0,0,0) = \vec 0
>>\implies (x,y,z) = (0,0,0)
>>Therefore x=y=z=0. Since all coefficients must be 0, it must be that the set of vectors are independent.
