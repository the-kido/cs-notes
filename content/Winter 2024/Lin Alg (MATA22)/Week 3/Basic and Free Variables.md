
>[!defn] Basic and Free variables
> In the [[  Row Echelon Form |echelon form]] of a linear system:
> - All the variables that appear as leading terms are <span class="emphasis">basic variables</span>.
> 	- I.e. all pivots (the first non-zero entry in a row) is basic. 
> - Everything else (including variables with a coefficient of 0) are <span class="emphasis">free variables</span>.
> 
> > [!example]
> >  ![[Pasted image 20250128191143.png]]
> >  - Basic variables: $x_1, x_2, x_4$
> >  - Free variables: $x_3, x_5$
