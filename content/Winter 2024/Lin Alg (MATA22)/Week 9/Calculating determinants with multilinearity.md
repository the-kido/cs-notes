>[!question] Suppose $A \in M_3(\mathbb R)$ and $\det(A) = 2$. What is $det(3A)$?
>>[!check] Answer
>>$det(3A) =$ a big 3x3 matrix multiplied by 3. That means the det will have 3 arguments for the 3 rows, each of which are multiplied by 3.
>>We know that $T(3a, 3b, 3c) = 3T(a, 3b, 3c) = \dots = 3^3T(a, b, c)$ by multilinearity of the determinant function.
>>So $det(3A) = 3^3\cdot 2 = 54$
