# Harmony stuff
- Cadence - how a phrase ends
	- authentic - resolved
	- plagal - resolved - amen
	- imperfect - unresolved
	- deceptive - unresolved - oh boy there's more
- Root / Quality Chord looks like $F^7$ or $G_{MI}^{(MA7)}$
	- It's a roman numeral and tells us the chords function and quality (I, ii, IV, etc)
	- Common ones:
	- `I` <span class="emphasis">Tonic Chord</span>
	- `ii` Supertonic
	- `IV` <span class="emphasis">Subdominant</span>
	- `V` <span class="emphasis">Dominant Chord</span>
	- `vi`: Submediant chord
- Common guys:
	- ii-V-I -> Jazz
	- V-ii-I -> Blues
	- I-vi-IV-V -> Pop/Rock
	- I-V-I -> Classical

- <span class="emphasis">Verse-Chorus/Refrain/Hook</span>
	- Usually in folk, pop, rock, R&B, hip-hop (only for hook)
	- Alternates between verse and other thing.
	- Chorus = the main part. catchy usually
	- Refrain = Very short but like chorus (Wind Cries Mary)
	- Hook = Rapped, not sang.
- Strophic = verse verse verse verse

#### Musicking
- Musicking: "To music is to take part, in any capacity, in a musical performance"
	- "To involve yourself in music"
- Art object = a work of art
	- Likewise a sound object is a work of music (like an audio file or disk)
- "Music" is not a thing, it's an activity <-- The FUNDAMENTAL theory here
- "Music" as we know could be ways of organizing sounds into meaning.
- Westerners like art object > art action
- Don't focus on the music object to find meaning; instead focus on the interactive nature of musical *performance*
	- "the meaning of music lies in what people do"
- No music is better than another music
	- Westerners believe in a "canon". 
- The purpose of "Musicking" is to divert your attention to the act of musical performances -- "an encounter between humans"
- "Everyone can music"
- The listener, performer, and composer all provide feedback to each other and are a part of "the creation of meaning" thru actions / skills / engagement
	- he thinks a linear comp -> perform -> listen model is wrong

#### Musical Embodiment by Tia DeNora
- Humans are *musically composed*
- We are a *socialized entity*
- <span class="emphasis">Embodied awareness</span>: our instincts, senses and intuition
	- See cup? Drink water
	- Raining? Take shelter
	- Sub-conscious
- Musical Entrainment
	- Reacting to music
	- Marching, tapping, singing, reacting to a fire alarm. that kinda thing
	- Aligning some bodily feature to a feature in our soundscape (beats especially)
	- Entrainment = synchronization of biological rhythms to external cues
- Newer music is easier to be musically entrained to
	- Think a pop song vs. nature music
- The aerobics case study
	- tl;dr: music structures the behavior of people!
	- Agency: The capacity to act and make decisions
	- In the gym, we use music to sustain ourselves over 45 minute sessions
	- She talks abt aerobatic order/disorder
		- order = motivation
		- disorder = distraction
	- There are 3 involved parties
		- The aerobics class members 
		- The instructors
		- The producers
	- This means the class members "active sense-makers" of the music, making it mean something for themselves for whatever reason (in this case, motivation)
	- <span class="emphasis">Disassociate Cognitive Strategies</span>
		- Not focusing on what you're doing to make a task easier
- Case Study: Music in the Neonatal Unit
	- <span class="emphasis">Intrauterine Symphony</span>
		- The sounds a baby hears in the uterus
		- Voices, heartbeats, etc.
		- Helps achieve homeostasis for the baby. Babies latch on to music in that sense
- Embodied awareness: What your body would correctly do with some specific thing occurs
	- When music ascends, a dancer might move *up*
- Primary signification: relationships *within* the music
	- like how 1 chord goes to another
- Secondary signification: how we make meaning for music
	- i.e. a song makes you remember a feeling from the past

#### Music for Visual Media
- AV = Media with audio and visual elements
	- AV can be electronic or live
- Mickey Mousing = when music aligns with what's happening on screen
	- tip toe = pizzicato type deal
- Soundtrack dissonance: music contradicting a scene

	- I.e. violin for birds. Drums for thunder

- Underscores: Music that happens under the action.
	- No one else hears it. 
	- AKA Incidental music
	- Think bg music in a video game. chemical plant!

- Grew up with nature as her soundscape. less of that now. she did learn the accordion and French horn tho!

	- They were good at hearing, not listening. ALSO hearing != listening
- The meditation project led to deep listening
- HEARING: letting sound into your hear. The physicals means that allow you to perceive sound
- LISTENING: Giving attention to those sounds.
	- U gotta be "conscious" abt listening. Hearing is *embodied awareness* in the words of Tia DeNora
- Deep Listening: Engagement with all facets of sound. 
	- "beyond ordinary boundaries"
- Improvisation: performing w/o reference
	- Breathing can be used to guide improv
- Toning: Giving "voice" to each breath. Making a breath audible
	- ahhh
- Sounding: improving with wacky sounds
	- Any sound made w/ the voice
- Two types of attention

	- Global attention: attention to the entire space/time continuum of sound
