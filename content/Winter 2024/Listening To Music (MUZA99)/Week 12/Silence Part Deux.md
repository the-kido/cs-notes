Silence is NECESSARY in music.
- It distinguishes music happening from not happening.
	- "tacet" --> no one playing at all.
	- Silence also distinguishes sound from each other, too.
- <span class="testable">Rests</span>: Symbols to denote silence in music
	- For specific durations of silences
- <span class="testable">Tacet</span>: Silence of an extending duration 
	- a G.P (general pause) in music notation refers to silence of one or more bars for the ENTIRE ensemble. 
	- Brass instruments ESPECIALLY have an indication for "tacet" for x number of bars. Usually cuz brass doesn't play the whole time.

Examples
- Beethoven (the line riders song; very aggressive)
- Lots of other ones

<span class="testable">Indeterminacy</span>: Where some parts / all parts of music are left to "chance"
- Aka aleatoric music
- e.g. improv is indeterminacy 
- <span class="emphasis">Chance music</span> is a kind of indeterminacy
	- <span class="emphasis">John Cage</span> leaves a LOT to chance
	- "his intention is to let things be themselves"
