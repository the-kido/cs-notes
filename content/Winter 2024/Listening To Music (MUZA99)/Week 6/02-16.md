### Range & contour
<span class="testable">Range</span>: the highest and lowest note of a piece 
<span class="testable">Countour</span>: The "shape" of a melody
- <span class="testable">Acsending</span>: a rising melody.
- <span class="testable">Decending</span>: a falling melody


### Melodic Motion
<span class="testable">Conjunct</span>: melodic motion which precedes by steps 
- No jumps. Is smooth and changes by 1 step (2nd intervals).
- Think ode to joy
- Usually vocal music is conjunt
<span class="testable">Disjunct</span>: melodic motion which proceeds by leaps (intervals of 3rds and larger; not steps)
- Think of the song where morty gets slapped. yep.
- Usually instrumentals are disjunct.
