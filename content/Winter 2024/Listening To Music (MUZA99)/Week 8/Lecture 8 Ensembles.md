## String Quartet
Haydn is very important in the history of string quartets!

<span class="testable">Conducting</span>
- Someone who leads an ensemble (performances and rehearsals!)
- Also does admin things (basically the face)
- AKA the Maestra/maestro

They could be the person leading with drums, the most experienced, the elder, etc. when we aren't talking abt the classical sense of a conductor

<span class="testable">Beat Pattern</span>
- A sequence of pulses the conductor gives to outline the tempo and metre
- A <span class="testable">Baton</span> is the funny stick they use to do that
- THE UPBEAT GOES UP. THE DOWNBEAT GOES DOWN. YOOO

<span class="testable">Opera</span>
- A drama that is sung accompanied by instruments
- Played in a theatre 
	- Jacopo Peri's Dafne (1597) was the first opera!
- The <span class="emphasis">Orchestra pit</span> is a bit pit where the orchestra plays
- <span class="testable">Staged opera</span>
	- An opera performed in full (acting, sets, staging, all there)
- <span class="testable">Concert Opera</span>
	- An opera as a concert w/ no actors or sets. Just singers
