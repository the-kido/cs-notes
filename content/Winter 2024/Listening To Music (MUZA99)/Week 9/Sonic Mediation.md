- <span class="testable">Hearing</span>
	- The physical perception of music.
	- frequencies have made their way into our eardrums and previeved by our brain
- <span class="testable">Listening</span>
	- Giving attention to what we hear. It is conscious 
- We cannot really reduce what we hear, but we can tune things out by reducing our attention to sound.
- <span class="testable">Deep Listening</span>
	- Practicing listening that requires active engagement that requires attention and not just to the music, but all facets of the sound.
	- "Depth" implies going beyond surface level listening; there is more complexity
		- How does the song relate to the sounds before and after it?
		- How does what you listen to relate to what else you could be hearing in the moment simultaneously?
		- The **space/time continuum of sound** is not testable cuz it's quite vague. We aren't exploring 1 sound, we're exploring *many* related sounds.
