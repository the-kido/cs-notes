## Lec 3
- Sound = very broad. Any auditory phenomena
- Noise = bad sounds (although white noise is also noise)
- Music = Organized sound
- Acoustics = the study of sound
- All sounds are vibrations
- Treble = higher-pitched sounds
- Bass = lower pitched sounds
- Frequency = Vibrations/cycles per second
- Pitch = how we perceive frequencies. 
- Tone = The sound of a definite pitch (indefinite pitches have high or low sounds, like rain)
- Timbre = the texture of sound
- Dynamics = volume
	- Forte = loud
	- Mezzo-forte = kinda loud
	- Mezzo-piano = kinda quiet
	- Piano = quiet
	- Niente = nothing
- 

Instrument time
- Orchestral families = sorted kinda arbitrarily
- Hornbostel / Sachs = sorted by how the sound is made 
- Woodwinds = aerophones; vibrating air column & reeds/apertures
	- Flute and Clarinet are like the two big ones 
	- Saxophone!
- Brass = also aerophones; sound made via buzzing lips
	- obvious
- Percussion (idiophone or membranophone) = anything that you hit to make a sound
	- Drums (membranophone). xylophone (idiophone)
- Strings = chordophones; strings vibrate. Bow or plucked  

Performance technique: infinite void
- Staccato
	- Notes played detached
- Legato
	- Notes played smoothly. Opposite of staccato
- Trill
	- Alternating between two points quickly
	- jaws theme
- Glissando
	- Notes played smoothly. A sliding movement over (usually) many notes
	- Rhapsody in Blue
- Arpeggio
	- Several notes / a chord played with notes in succession rather than together at once 
- Bend
	- Better call Saul (bending between two points)
- Riff
	- A reoccurring phrase. 
- Drone
	- A note held continuously
- Tacet
	- Silence
- Vibrato
	- opera singing.

#### Musicking
1. Music is an activity

3. A musical performance is a social thing with many people!
4. Art object = a work of art (i.e. a painting)
5. Music object = a work of music (i.e. a musical recording)

- octave = 8 span

	- Chromatic scale = all 12 notes
	- Major scale = happy

- Contour = shape of melody
